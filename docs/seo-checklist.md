# SEO & sharing checklist

## Once

- [ ] **Settings → Meta tags → Search description** on, with a ~150-character site description.
- [ ] **Settings → Publishing**: custom domain + HTTPS redirect (see `custom-domain.md`).
- [ ] **Google Search Console**: verify `freakinfood.com` (DNS TXT record at Tratto), submit `https://www.freakinfood.com/sitemap.xml`.

## Every post

- [ ] One section label: **Recipes**, **Drinks**, **Stories** or **Reviews** plus any topic labels; add **Highlight** to feature it on the homepage.
- [ ] Dish and place names in their own spelling (bún chả, not bun cha).
- [ ] **Search description** in the post settings sidebar, one sentence.
- [ ] **Permalink → Custom**: short, e.g. `bun-cha-hanoi`.
- [ ] **Alt text** on every image. The first image becomes the card thumbnail and share image, so make it the best one.
- [ ] Recipes: paste `snippets/recipe-card.html`, fill it in, run the [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) on the published URL.

## Pinterest

- [ ] Pinterest business account → Settings → **Claimed accounts** → claim `www.freakinfood.com` (meta tag in the theme `<head>`, or a DNS TXT record at Tratto).
- [ ] Recipe posts with the JSON-LD are eligible for recipe Rich Pins.
- [ ] Make pins from the 1000 × 1500 templates in the social kit and link each one to the post URL.

## Instagram & Flipboard

- [ ] Instagram bio link → homepage, or a `/p/links.html` page.
- [ ] Flipboard magazine (@Freakinfood): flip each new post in. The 1200 × 630 template doubles as the share image.

# Social kit

Templates for Instagram, Pinterest and Flipboard live in a Claude Design canvas, "FreakinFood Social Kit". Ask the owner for the share link (it's private until shared).

| Template | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram recipe post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | New recipe announcement |
| Instagram quote post | 1080 × 1350 | Food-map finds, quotes from a story |
| Instagram carousel cover + step | 1080 × 1350 | Step-by-step recipes |
| Instagram story | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Keep top 250 px and bottom 340 px clear |
| Pinterest recipe pin | 1000 × 1500 (2:3) | One per recipe post |
| Pinterest food-map pin | 1000 × 1500 | City guides |
| Flipboard cover / link preview | 1200 × 630 | Flipboard magazine and share image |
| Avatar & highlight covers | 1080 × 1080 | Instagram profile |

Export finished images into `assets/social/` if you want them versioned here.

# Assets

Put brand files here:

- `logo/` — full-colour horizontal lockup (default), stacked, white, one-colour ink, app icons. Take them from the FreakinFood design system's **Logos** group. Keep the mark's own inks (`#9c4326`, `#154212`) inside the mark only.
- `favicon/` — 32 × 32 and 180 × 180 PNG from the app-icon tile. Upload in Blogger under **Layout → Favicon**.
- `social/` — exported images from the social kit.

Blogger hosts images itself: upload them in Blogger, then reference the Blogger URL. Files here are the originals.
